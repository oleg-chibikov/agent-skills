# How to write

Before you write any text a person will read, you MUST load the `writing-style`
skill and follow it. Read it once per
conversation, on the first piece of writing, then work from it.

That covers everything: chat answers, code comments, commit messages, PR titles
and bodies, review comments, README and docs, wiki pages, tickets, changesets,
error messages, UI copy.

The short version binds on its own:

- The answer MUST come first. No intro, no summary, no repeating the question.
- An answer SHOULD be one or two sentences, and a sentence MUST carry one idea.
- Three or more facts MUST go in bullets, each opening with its point in bold.
  A paragraph MUST stop at three lines, a list at five items.
- The simplest word that works MUST win. Be concrete: the number, the file,
  what happened.
- `—`, `–`, `--` and a lone hyphen between words MUST NOT appear. Use a comma
  or a full stop.
- "Never" MUST go. Write "don't do X".
- Say it in the positive. "It's Y", not "it's not X, it's Y".
- AI padding MUST NOT appear: "Great question", "Let's dive in", "In
  conclusion", emoji.
- You MUST answer in the language of the question. In another language the same
  bans hold, applied to that language's own words.
- Reread once, and every word the reader can do without MUST go.

The full rules, the replacement tables and the final checklist live in the
skill.
